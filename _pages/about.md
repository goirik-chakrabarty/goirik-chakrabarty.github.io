Hi there!👋 I’m Goirik Chakrabarty, a Ph.D. candidate in Data Science at the University of Göttingen. My research focuses on neuroscience data and mouse visual cortex. For the machine learning folks I want to develop a multimodal foundation model replicating the mouse visual cortex, this entails connecting vision and time-series data which is very learning and partially correlated. I work with deep learning, computer vision, diffusion models, transformers and time-series analysis, and I have practical experience with high-performance computing and large, complex datasets.
<!-- I aim to develop general methods that provide value across many fields. -->

Prior to this, I worked as a Pre-Doctoral Researcher at TCS Research under [Ramya Hebbalaguppe](https://rhebbalaguppe.github.io/) in collaboration with [Prathosh A.P. (IISc Bangalore)](https://sites.google.com/view/prathosh). Here I worked on vision-language models🤖, for image editing.

I completed my MS Thesis titled,"[Continual Domain Incremental Learning during Test-time](http://dr.iiserpune.ac.in:8080/xmlui/bitstream/handle/123456789/7830/20181079_Goirik_MS_Thesis.pdf?sequence=1)" under the supervision of [Soma Biswas (IISc Bangalore)](https://sites.google.com/iisc.ac.in/somabiswas) and [Leelavati Narlikar (IISER Pune)](https://www.iiserpune.ac.in/research/department/data-science/people/faculty/regular-faculty/leelavati-narlikar/355).
